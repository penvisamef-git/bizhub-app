BizHub runs every part of your small business from one app. Make a digital name card, open a café or shop, sell event tickets or share notes with your team, then manage it all from your phone, in English or Khmer.

One account, many businesses. Add only what you need and pay only for what you use with BizHub Coins.

WHAT YOU CAN BUILD

• Digital name card: a beautiful card with your phone, email, Telegram, website and map. Share it by link or QR code. Choose from 100+ designs in your own colour and see how many people opened it.

• Website: a simple website for your business with your logo, photos, products and contact details. No coding needed.

• Menu QR: put your menu on a QR code. Customers scan it at the table and see photos, prices and options in Khmer or English.

• Flip book: turn a PDF catalogue, brochure or price list into a flip book that opens on any phone.

• QR generator: create QR codes for links, Wi-Fi, phone numbers and more, ready to print.

• Restaurant POS: take orders by table, send them to the kitchen, print or share receipts and see today's sales in real time.

• Mini mart: sell with a barcode scanner, keep track of stock, manage products and prices, and take orders.

• Guest house: rooms, bookings, check-in and check-out, guests and payments in one place.

• Staff attendance: staff check in and out with a QR code. See who is in, who is late and who is not in yet.

• Event tickets: publish an event page, sell ticket types, take KHQR payments and check tickets at the door by scanning their QR code.

• Debt book: write down who owes you and who you owe, with reminders and a clear history.

• Digital Note: rich notes with checklists, due dates, files, stickers and quotes. Share by link or QR, collect replies and sign-ups, and put notes in groups.

• Loyalty stamp card: give customers a digital stamp card. They collect stamps with each visit and get a reward.

ONE DASHBOARD

• Sales today, this week and this month across all your businesses.
• Top selling items, open bills, guests in house and staff at work at a glance.

Your wallet shows every purchase and every spend.

MADE FOR CAMBODIA

• Full Khmer and English, switch at any time.
• Prices in US dollars or riel.
• Your customers can pay you with KHQR (event tickets, restaurant and shop).
• Light and dark mode.

SAFE AND SIMPLE

• Sign in with email or Google.
• Your data is sent over encrypted connections.
• You can delete your account and data at any time from the app or at bizhubcambodia.com/delete-account.

## Before you paste: check these against the app

- **Feature claims.** Every line above is meant to match what BizHub really does today. Remove any line for a feature that isn't live yet (for example barcode scanning in Mini mart, debt reminders, or Flip book), because Google rejects listings that promise missing features.
- **"riel".** Keep "Prices in US dollars or riel" only if businesses can really set KHR prices.
- **Coins wording.** In the Android app coins are bought only with Google Play billing (same packs and USD prices as the web). Don't mention KHQR or bank top-up for coins in the listing or in the app. KHQR stays in the text only for **event tickets**, where the business's own customers pay for a real event (a physical service, allowed by Google).
- **No developer name on the images.** The screenshots and feature graphic show only the BizHub name and logo.
